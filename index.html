Approach

Pricing details

Pay-As-You-Go. Billed per session, so there's no period total — you pay for the sessions you book.

Foundation. Billed upfront based on the sessions we'll do that period — already accounts for any known holidays or vacation. At 5x per week, that comes to $1,125 per week.

Performance. Billed upfront based on the sessions we'll do that period — already accounts for any known holidays or vacation. At 5x per week, that comes to about $4,330 per month. The exact amount depends on how many sessions are scheduled that month.

Executive Performance. Billed annually, based on 50 training weeks a year — your trainer's 2 weeks of vacation aren't billed. You'll also get limited virtual check-ins during that time to keep you on track. At 5x per week, that comes to $43,750 per year.

Executive Performance, training 4x or 5x per week. You also get guaranteed holiday coverage — your session still happens, in person if your trainer's in town or virtual if traveling — and sick days are fully covered: if your trainer's out sick, that session is never billed and never owed back.
